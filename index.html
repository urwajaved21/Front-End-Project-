FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can attend?

Any student on campus. You don't need to be from a computer science department, and beginners are welcome at the bootcamps.

Does it cost anything?

No. All events are free, but seats are limited so you need to register.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

Yes for the hackathons and bootcamps. Lectures don't need one.

Can I join a hackathon alone?

Yes. If you register without a team, we'll help you find one at the kickoff.

Can I register for more than one event?

Yes, as long as the times don't overlap. Check the agenda before you pick.

What if I can't make it after registering?

Please cancel by emailing the organizers so your seat can go to someone on the waitlist.
